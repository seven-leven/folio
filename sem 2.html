Introduction & Concept

The project explores the intersection of artistic practice and childhood play, proposing a new typology of public infrastructure that challenges traditional art education norms.

Site Analysis & Strategy

Form Development: Ricci Flow Study

The "Ricci Flow" concept served as a foundational design study for this project. It explores an iterative process of form generation (illustrated below) which guided the organic development of the main building's structure (showcased by the hero image) and offered insights into creating adaptable, evolving spaces. This methodology also presents potential for further exploration in smaller-scale elements, such as furniture design (a related sub-study not detailed on this page).
