06

Play

More movement than the rest of the site, with limits

Allowed

  • A drawing card lifts 4 px and tilts up to 1°
  • The drawing inside grows up to 6%
  • Canvas animations that loop quietly
  • A glow on a milestone dot

Not allowed

  • Tilt or scaling on anything else
  • Bounce or elastic easing
  • Sound, or anything that flashes
  • Any of this on an academic or professional page

Every animation waits until it is on screen, and stops for reduced motion.
