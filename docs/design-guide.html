03
Urban Acupuncture

Always

  • One bird per semester, used once per medium.
  • As drawn: its own colours, its own direction.
  • Site: last in the layer order. Text, tiles and the lead image sit over it; it never covers anything.
  • Site: 26% strength, 13 to 25 rem tall, never taller than the text beside it. Phone: same, flush right.
  • Book: 100% strength, outer half of the opener, 5 mm clear of all text. May bleed.
  • File: white paper removed, WebP, 760 px at most, alt="".

Never

  • Mirrored, recoloured, outlined, boxed or cropped into.
  • On the homepage, navbar, footer, cover or chapter pages.
  • Clickable, zoomable or animated.
  • Two birds in one view.
